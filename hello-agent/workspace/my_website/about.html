How We Started

Bridging the Gap Between Pet Expectations & Reality

Too many pets end up in shelters simply because families didn't realize a breed's exercise demands, shedding habits, or training needs before bringing them home.

We founded BREED to provide clear, transparent, and objective breed profiles. By helping future owners understand energy ratings, kid-friendliness, and health considerations before adopting or buying, we help form lifelong bonds.
